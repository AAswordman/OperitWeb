# Operit 2 Pre-release Notes

## Downloads and releases

Operit 2 is a **cross-platform product**, not limited to iOS and macOS.

- **iOS public beta is available**: [join through TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/hzq2xRrH).
- **macOS public beta is available**: [join through TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/hzq2xRrH).
- **Other platforms are in private testing**: access will open gradually. Follow official announcements for details.

iOS and macOS share the TestFlight invitation link above. You can also select a platform on the [Operit 2 download page](/#/v2/download).

Public beta is not a general release. Platform availability and further release plans follow official announcements. Operit 1 APKs are not Operit 2 installers.

## Guides and data

Generation-two guides are maintained independently. Confirmed installation, configuration and migration steps will be added later. Generation-one tutorials should not be treated as generation-two instructions.

Until official migration guidance is published, do not assume backup formats or configuration files can be imported between generations.

## Plugin ecosystem

Both generations use the same [plugin market](/#/market) and account. There is no separate generation-two catalog. Individual plugins still depend on their declared app-version requirements.

To use the currently available first generation, open [Operit 1 downloads](/#/v1/download) and [guides](/#/v1/guide).
